QUICK REFERENCE

Understanding common readings

General adult references
♥

Pulse

60–100 BPM is a commonly used resting reference for adults. Activity, stress, medication, and health conditions can change it.

◌

SpO₂

Many healthy readings are 95–100%. Altitude, circulation, device fit, and personal conditions affect interpretation.

⌁

Blood pressure

Below 120/80 mmHg is a general reference. A clinician considers repeated measurements and your health context.

∿

ECG

ECG interpretation depends on waveform features and clinical context; a single number cannot confirm heart health.

!

When to seek urgent help

Seek urgent professional care for severe chest discomfort, major breathing difficulty, fainting, or severe weakness. This page cannot assess emergencies.
